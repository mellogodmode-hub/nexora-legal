Nexora - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Nexora is a Discord bot that provides moderation, ticket management, and server management features. This policy explains how information may be processed when you use Nexora.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled by a Discord server, Nexora may process:

  • Discord user, server, channel, and message IDs.
  • Moderation records, including warnings, timeouts, action timestamps, and moderator IDs.
  • Messages and information submitted through support tickets.
  • Server configuration, including selected channels and role IDs.
  • Technical logs needed to diagnose errors and maintain the service.

Please do not submit passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information through tickets.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate bot commands, manage support tickets, apply moderation actions, save server settings, troubleshoot errors, protect the service, and respond to data requests.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Storage and Retention

Depending on the bot's implementation, information may be stored in databases, configuration files, or technical logs. Information should only be retained for as long as necessary for its purpose or as required by law. Actual retention and deletion practices depend on the bot's configuration and hosting environment.

5. Third-Party Services

Nexora uses Discord's platform and API. Discord processes information under its own policies. The bot may also rely on hosting or database providers to operate.

Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy

6. Your Requests

You may contact the Nexora developer through the official support server to ask about information associated with you or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable law and legitimate security or moderation needs.

7. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Nexora must only be used in accordance with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable law.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when Nexora's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Nexora developer through the official Nexora support server.

Before publishing, the developer must verify that this policy accurately describes the bot's actual data collection, storage, and deletion practices.
